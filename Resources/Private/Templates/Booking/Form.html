The booking form of an event: the upcoming dates with their free seats (a radio list for recurring events, a single date otherwise), the participant fields, the privacy checkbox and the spam protection. Variables: event (Event), availabilities (OccurrenceAvailability[]), bookable (bool, whether any date can be booked), bookingRequest (BookingRequest), frontendUser (fe_users row or empty array), settings (plugin settings; settings.booking holds the BookingSettings of the event).

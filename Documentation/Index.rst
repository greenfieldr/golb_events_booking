Golb Events Booking - Book Events The Page Way
===============================================

----

Golb Events Booking lets visitors book the dates of the event pages of
Golb Events. Booking works with and without frontend users: logged in
users book with their account, guests book with an email address and
confirm the booking through a link. Bookings are records in a storage
folder with capacity per date, waiting list, booking window and
notifications.
